PROGRAM

You are not learning English. You are learning to explain your engineering in English.

Star Speaker is a practical speaking program designed for engineers who understand English but freeze when they need to speak at work.

Before and after the program

Before

  • Struggling to begin a sentence
  • Being unable to access words you already know
  • Giving short, uncertain answers
  • Making your technical knowledge sound weaker than it is

After

  • Starting your answer calmly and clearly
  • Developing your idea with a natural structure
  • Explaining technical topics in an understandable way
  • Showing your expertise with confidence

HOW DOES IT WORK?

Progress does not happen by chance. It happens with the right system.

Star Speaker identifies your speaking problem, then gives you focused practice, expert feedback, and repetition in real work scenarios.

  1. 01

    Identify the real problem

    Find where you freeze, why you lose clarity, and what you need to focus on when speaking.

  2. 02

    Practice speaking every day

    Instead of passive lessons, complete short, focused speaking tasks prepared for your needs.

  3. 03

    Improve with expert feedback

    Focus on one important weakness at a time and see clearly what to improve and how.

  4. 04

    Prepare with real work scenarios

    Rehearse job interviews, meetings, technical explanations, and presentations.

  5. 05

    See your progress in your recordings

    Compare your first and final recordings to make the real change in your speaking visible.
