HOW DOES IT WORK?

Progress does not happen by accident. It happens through the right system.

Star Speaker identifies your speaking problem, then gives you focused practice, expert feedback, and repetition in real career scenarios.

  1. 01

    Identify the real problem

    Find where you freeze, why you lose clarity, and what you need to focus on when speaking.

  2. 02

    Practice speaking every day

    Instead of passive lessons, complete short, focused speaking tasks prepared for your needs.

  3. 03

    Improve with expert feedback

    Focus on one important weakness at a time and see clearly what to improve and how.

  4. 04

    Prepare with real career scenarios

    Rehearse interviews, meetings, presentations, and international work situations.

  5. 05

    See your progress in your recordings

    Compare your first and final recordings to make the real change in your speaking visible.
